# AeroFit – Customer Profiling & Product Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer characteristics and purchasing behaviour for AeroFit, a fitness equipment company offering different treadmill models.

The objective is to identify customer segments for each treadmill product and understand how demographic, financial, and fitness-related factors influence product preferences.

The analysis uses exploratory data analysis (EDA), descriptive statistics, data visualization, and probability analysis to generate actionable business insights.

## Business Problem

AeroFit wants to understand the characteristics of customers purchasing its three treadmill models: KP281, KP481, and KP781.

The market research team aims to identify differences in customer profiles across these products to improve product recommendations, customer targeting, and marketing strategies.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics and purchasing patterns.
- Understand customer preferences across treadmill models.
- Identify relationships between income, age, fitness, usage, and product selection.
- Calculate marginal and conditional probabilities.
- Develop customer profiles for individual treadmill products.
- Provide data-driven marketing and business recommendations.

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains 180 customer records and 9 attributes.

| Attribute | Description |

| Education | Years of education |
| MaritalStatus | Customer marital status |

## Key Findings

### Product Preferences

- KP281 accounts for approximately 44.4% of purchases.
- KP481 accounts for approximately 33.3% of purchases.
- KP781 accounts for approximately 22.2% of purchases.
- KP281 is the most frequently purchased model.

### Customer Demographics

- Approximately 57.8% of customers are male and 42.2% are female.
- The largest customer age concentration is between 25 and 30 years.
- Most customers have an annual income between 50,000 and 60,000.

### Customer Segmentation

- KP281 attracts customers with comparatively lower income and fitness levels.
- KP481 appeals to a broader customer segment.
- KP781 is preferred by customers with higher income and fitness levels.

### Fitness-Based Product Preferences

- Customers in the below-average fitness category represent the largest segment.
- KP281 and KP481 have stronger representation among customers with lower and moderate fitness levels.
- KP781 has a stronger association with customers in the excellent fitness category.

## Business Recommendations

- Position KP281 as an accessible option for beginner fitness enthusiasts.
- Promote KP481 as a balanced option for regular fitness users.
- Position KP781 as a premium product for serious fitness enthusiasts.
- Develop targeted marketing campaigns based on age, income, and fitness levels.
- Introduce product bundles and loyalty programs to improve customer engagement.
- Use customer characteristics to improve personalized product recommendations.

## Conclusion

This case study demonstrates how exploratory data analysis and probability techniques can help businesses understand customer behaviour and product preferences.

The findings provide insights into customer segmentation, product positioning, and targeted marketing strategies that can support better business decision-making.
